Questions about pricing

Honest answers.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The free tier requires no credit card and has no time limit. You enter a card only when you upgrade to Pro or Pro Annual.

The free tier has hard limits: 20 materials, 20 products, 1 channel, 30 orders/month. Those limits exist so you can verify that auto-deduction works correctly before paying anything. They are not there to hide the product behind a trial clock.

Can I cancel at any time?

Monthly plans: yes. Canceling stops billing at the end of your current billing period. You keep Pro access through the date you paid for. No cancellation fee, no required notice period. This is the billing intent, not a guarantee carved out by fine print.

Annual plans: canceling stops the automatic renewal. The current year’s plan stays active through the paid date. Annual billing is not prorated on cancellation — the $180 covers the full year you paid for.

What happens when I hit 30 orders on the free tier?

Orders above the 30/month cap are queued and not automatically deducted. You see them in your sync dashboard; materials are not silently lost or silently doubled. You will receive an email notification when you hit the cap.

Upgrading to Pro processes the queued orders. An Etsy seller running more than 30 orders a month is generating real revenue; $19/month to automate material tracking at that volume is less than $0.64 per order.

Is the price the same on the Shopify App Store?

Yes. $19/month on every channel, including the Shopify App Store.

Inventora lists $23/month on the Shopify App Store (apps.shopify.com/inventora) while showing $19/month on their own website (inventora.com/pricing-plans). BatchCOGS charges the same price everywhere.

Does the $180/year annual plan auto-renew?

Yes. The annual plan renews at $180 each year unless you cancel before the renewal date. You will receive a renewal reminder 14 days before the charge. Canceling before that date stops the next charge and keeps access through the paid year.

Does BatchCOGS track labor or overhead, or only materials?

Material costs only at launch — the raw inputs in your recipe. Labor and overhead are not auto-calculated in the current version. You can add a manual overhead line in the COGS view for an approximate fully-loaded cost, but the number is not tracked against orders.

This is the honest scope of the current build. Labor and overhead tracking are on the roadmap; they are not in the product today.

How does BatchCOGS compare to Stocksmith Indie beyond price?

Stocksmith (formerly Craftybase) is an established product with 245 reviews at 4.6/5 on Capterra (capterra.com). That review record reflects years of real use. BatchCOGS does not have that record yet.

What Stocksmith Indie does that BatchCOGS does not: equipment depreciation tracking, multi-user accounts, more advanced reporting at higher tiers. The core feature — recipe-based auto-deduction on Etsy and Shopify orders — is present in both. Stocksmith Indie charges $990/year for it. BatchCOGS Pro Annual charges $180/year.
