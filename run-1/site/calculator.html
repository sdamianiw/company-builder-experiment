From calculator to live tracking

This calculator is a one-time estimate. BatchCOGS keeps it current.

Your cost per unit changes every time you restock materials at a different price. In BatchCOGS, you update the cost once and every recipe that uses that material recalculates automatically. When an order arrives on Etsy or Shopify, materials deduct from stock and COGS is recorded per sale.

Recipe-based auto-deduction

Link materials to each product in a recipe. Every order on Etsy or Shopify deducts the exact materials from your stock — no spreadsheet update required.

COGS per sale, not per batch

BatchCOGS logs the materials cost at the moment each order deducts. Your COGS report shows actual cost per unit sold, updated with each material price change.

Stock you can trust

Every webhook deduction carries an idempotency key — a duplicate order event does not deduct twice. If a deduction ever looks wrong, the audit log shows every change and you can correct it manually.
