**Claim under test:** Solo massage therapists on Square/Fresha are forced into $25–50/month full practice-management suites solely to get SOAP notes and intake forms integrated with client profiles.

**Wedge:** Square Appointments (free) and Fresha (free) have literally zero SOAP note or intake form functionality. No standalone $5–15/month SOAP-note tool with Square/Fresha integration exists.

## Fresh Incumbent Search — Key Finding: Fresha Weakens the Zero-Documentation Claim

Fresha's features page (fetched directly) lists:
- **"Advanced notes"** — "Store detailed notes, treatment records, and body charts so nothing gets missed between visits"
- **"Custom forms"** — "Collect important information before appointments with digital forms clients complete in advance"
- **"Digital consent"** — digital signatures, secure storage
- **"Client profiles"** — including allergies, patch tests, forms, notes

Fresha's help center returned **no results for "SOAP notes"**, confirming the format is general (not structured S/O/A/P). However, the built-in custom forms + advanced notes + body charts could serve the clinical documentation function for therapists who don't require strict SOAP format compliance.

**Impact:** The wedge claim that Fresha has "literally zero clinical documentation capability" is **overstated**. Fresha has general documentation features that may partially serve this need, though not in SOAP format.

## ClinicSense Square Integration — Critical Nuance

ClinicSense appears in the Square App Marketplace. However, the integration is **payment-only**: users process invoices through Square payment hardware within ClinicSense. There is no sync of Square Appointments bookings into ClinicSense.

The integration effectively requires **migrating scheduling away from Square Appointments** to ClinicSense — it does not allow "stay on Square Appointments + add ClinicSense SOAP notes." The wedge's framing holds: there is no cheap add-on that plugs SOAP notes into Square Appointments.

## Summary Assessment

**What holds:** Square Appointments gap is real and independently evidenced. Full-suite price floor is confirmed ($33–39/month). No true Square Appointments + SOAP notes integration at low price exists.

**What's weak:** The "Fresha = zero documentation" assertion is inaccurate — Fresha has built-in custom forms, advanced notes, and body charts on its free plan. Therapists already on Fresha may partially solve the problem without switching. The wedge is strongest for Square Appointments users; weaker for Fresha users.

**Verdict: WEAK** — Core Square gap is well-evidenced, but Fresha's built-in documentation features create a partial incumbent solution that undermines the "both platforms have zero capability" framing. The opportunity is real but narrower than claimed (primarily a Square Appointments gap). Story strength moderate.
