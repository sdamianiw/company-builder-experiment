**Claim**: Independent landlords managing 1–30 units track maintenance via texts/email, lose repair history, and fail to update tenants — because Buildium ($55+/mo) is priced for portfolio managers and free tools lack a work-order workflow.

**Verdict**: REFUTED

## 2. The 68% Statistic — Unverifiable

The only web source found for "68% of small landlords use phone calls, texts, and handwritten notes for maintenance" is:
- **ScreenForge Labs** (screenforgelabs.com/blog/maintenance-request-systems-from-chaos-to-organized), published June 5, 2026
- ScreenForge Labs attributes the stat to "a 2023 survey by the National Association of Residential Property Managers (NARPM)"
- **No direct link or citation** to the original NARPM survey is provided
- The claim cannot be independently verified against a primary source
- NARPM's own website and press center do not surface this specific 68% figure in search results
- ScreenForge Labs is a content marketing site — this appears to be a statistic propagating through secondary marketing content without a traceable primary source

**Assessment**: The 68% statistic is UNVERIFIABLE and likely either fabricated or severely misattributed. It should not be treated as evidence.

## 3. The Core Technical Claim: "Free Tools Lack a Work-Order Workflow"

This is the most important thing to refute, and the evidence is clear: **the claim is false**.

### Innago (100% FREE — no monthly fee, no per-unit fee, no setup fee)
- Tenant maintenance request portal with photo/video upload
- Work order tracking and management
- Contractor/vendor assignment (integrates with Latchel vendor network)
- Tenant status updates and notifications through the app
- Repair history by unit
- Covers the EXACT broken loop described: tenant submits → assigned to contractor → tenant notified

### TurboTenant (Free basic tier)

- Help Center article explicitly titled "Work Orders on Maintenance Requests" (support.turbotenant.com)

### Avail (Free tier)

### TenantCloud (Free tier available)

**The claim that "free tools lack a work-order workflow" is factually incorrect.** Innago alone — which is genuinely free — provides the complete workflow described as missing: request intake, work order creation, contractor assignment, tenant notification, history tracking.

## 5. Recency Assessment

- Buildium pricing pain is REAL and current (6 price increases in 2.5 years per user complaints as of 2026, now starting at $62/month)
- Innago has been free with work orders for years — this is not a 2025-2026 opportunity
- The underlying behavior (landlords using texts/email) may persist not because tools don't exist, but because adoption is the actual barrier — a different and harder problem
- The article from riooapp.com (March 2026) acknowledges the workflow problem exists but it also exists within the context of multiple free solutions being available

---

## 6. Buildability Assessment

**One person, one month**: Technically possible to build a basic maintenance request tracker. However:

- **No moat**: Any MVP would immediately compete with Innago (free, established, well-reviewed), TurboTenant, Avail
- **Trust problem**: Small landlords trying new software face switching costs; established free tools have years of reviews and integrations
- **The real barrier is adoption, not feature gap**: Landlords who use texts/email do so out of habit, not because they can't find software
- **Contractor network integration is hard**: The value of Thumbtack/Latchel integrations cannot be replicated in a month
- **Verdict**: Could build it, but would immediately be obsolete versus free incumbents

---

## Summary of Refutation Points

1. **Quote source compromised**: The Buildium complaint comes from a competitor's own marketing page
2. **68% statistic unverifiable**: No primary source found; originates only from content marketing sites without citations
3. **Core gap claim false**: Innago is free and provides complete work-order workflow including contractor assignment and tenant notifications
4. **Market saturated**: 4+ free options, 3+ cheap options directly serve this exact need
5. **Pain is real; gap is not**: Small landlords do struggle with maintenance tracking, but the software solutions exist — adoption/habit is the actual problem, which is a much harder startup problem
6. **Not buildable to differentiation**: A one-month build cannot compete with established free tools on features, trust, or network effects
