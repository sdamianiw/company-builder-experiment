## Ranking Rationale

**Rank 1 (Solo LMT SOAP notes):** Only candidate with severity 8/10. Six named complainants across two independent sources. Wedge is binary and undeniable — Square and Fresha have literally zero SOAP note functionality. Buildability is very high (SOAP note templates + intake forms + PDF export = standard CRUD). Launch video writes itself: a therapist paying $40/month for a suite she hates, just to keep clinical records attached to client profiles.

**Rank 2 (Square deposit-at-booking):** Nine named complainants across three sources over three years is the strongest evidence density of any candidate. Storytelling hook is visceral ("I prepped and waited and nobody showed up"). Docked because the gap analysis shows this is ecosystem inertia: users know Acuity and Cal.com exist but won't run two systems. Correct form is a Square App Marketplace plugin requiring Square partnership — this is a distribution constraint, not a product constraint.

**Rank 3 (Etsy COGS):** Confirmed by primary reviews across two platforms. The 5x price hike is a strong triggering event. Docked because the gap is reliability + trust + discoverability rather than a missing product category — Ardent Seller may already solve this if discovered.

**Rank 4 (Prompt versioning):** Severity is real (5/10) but evidence has a researcher-acknowledged caveat: no direct proof users tried Fabric/Langfuse and rejected them. HN usernames (tra3, runjake) are pseudonymous. Wedge is partly inferred.

**Rank 5 (Slack nonprofit migration):** Emotionally compelling story and confirmed event, but structurally weak for recurring SaaS: one-time event per org means churn is guaranteed. Included as a potential one-time tool or freemium community-building play, not a subscription product.
