# Pain Candidates — Top 8

**Generated:** 2026-07-11  
**Research scope:** 7 angles (reddit-smb, reddit-consumer, hackernews, reviews, niche-pro, ducttape, new-pains)  
**Scoring method:** Severity × frequency-of-complaint × underserved-ness × buildability-in-one-month-by-software  

## Clustering Notes (Before Ranking)

Near-duplicates merged into single clusters:

- **Freelancer financial admin cluster**: Harvest 10x–100x repricing (reviews), Wave paywall 2025 (reddit-smb), and late invoice chasing (reddit-smb) all point to the same missing product — a flat-rate time-tracking + invoicing + auto-reminder combo at ≤$15/month. Ranked as one pain.
- **Small landlord maintenance cluster**: niche-pro landlord pain and ducttape landlord maintenance runner-up are the same workflow gap. Combined.
- **Freelancer mini-CRM** (reddit-smb client pipeline) and **consultant proposal tracker** (ducttape) are near-identical personas and solutions. Kept outside top 8 because the merged cluster scored below the landlord and subscription-tracker pains on underserved-ness (HoneyBook, Bonsai, Clientjoy partially address this).
- **Hootsuite / Mailchimp / Typeform** pricing pains were considered but ranked below top 8: Buffer/Publer address Hootsuite; MailerLite/Brevo address Mailchimp; Tally.so addresses Typeform. The underserved gap is lower than the top 8.
